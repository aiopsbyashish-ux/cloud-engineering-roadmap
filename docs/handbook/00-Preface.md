Why I Wrote This Book ?

Throughout my career in IT infrastructure, I have worked across enterprise technologies including storage, backup, virtualization, operating systems, and cloud platforms. Like many infrastructure professionals, I reached a stage where I wanted to transition into Cloud Engineering and, eventually, Cloud Architecture.

During this journey, I realized that finding the right learning resources was more difficult than learning the technology itself.

Most books, online courses, and tutorials are designed for one of two audiences. They either assume that the reader is a complete beginner with little or no infrastructure experience, or they assume the reader is already working as a Cloud or DevOps engineer. Very few resources are written for experienced infrastructure professionals who already understand enterprise IT but need guidance on how to connect that knowledge with modern cloud technologies.

This book was created to bridge that gap.

It is written for professionals who have spent years working with enterprise infrastructure and now want to transition into Cloud Engineering without starting their learning journey from scratch. Rather than treating the reader as a beginner, this book builds upon existing knowledge of storage, backup, virtualization, networking, operating systems, and enterprise operations, and uses those familiar concepts to explain cloud technologies in a practical and relatable way.

This book is also a record of my own learning journey. Every concept, diagram, lab, and project has been created while learning and practicing the technologies myself. Instead of simply documenting commands, the goal is to explain why technologies work the way they do, how they are used in enterprise environments, and how they fit together to build modern cloud platforms.

I hope this book becomes a practical reference not only for me but also for professionals who have years of infrastructure experience, limited time to learn, and the ambition to grow into Cloud Engineering and Cloud Architecture roles.

If this book helps even one experienced engineer make that transition with greater confidence, then its purpose will have been fulfilled.

Mission:

This book is dedicated to every Infrastructure Engineer who has ever thought, "I know enterprise infrastructure, but I don't know where to start with cloud."

— Ashish Jain
